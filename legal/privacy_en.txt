CARD RUSH — PRIVACY POLICY (DRAFT)
Version: draft 0.2 · Developer: Poyraz Yazılım
This text is a draft and must be legally reviewed before publication.

1. Account and local data
Card Rush does not require an account. Your player name, avatar, settings, statistics, coins and collection are stored only on your device and are not sent to the developer.

2. Multiplayer
When you join a room, your player name, avatar number and game moves are sent to the hosting device and the players in the room. On a local network the connection is direct between devices. Internet rooms use a matchmaking (signaling) server only to set up the connection; it sees a hash of the room code and connection setup data (including IP addresses), not game content. If a direct connection is impossible, traffic may pass through a relay (TURN) server.

3. Voice chat
The microphone is used only while you switch it on; the screen shows "Mic on" while it is live. Your voice is not recorded or stored; it is only streamed live to players in the same room. With the mic off, no audio is captured or sent.

4. Ads
Mobile versions show ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may process device identifiers such as the advertising ID and ad interaction data under Google's policies. Where required, your consent is asked before ads; you can change your choices in Settings. Buying Premium stops all ad requests.

5. Purchases
Purchases are made through Google Play or the App Store; your payment details are not shared with us.

6. Children
Card Rush is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.
